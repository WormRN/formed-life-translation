Philippians
Formed Life Translation — v0.9 RC1

Chapter 1

This letter is from Paul and Timothy, servants of Christ Jesus. We are writing to all of God’s holy people in Philippi who are united with Christ Jesus, including your church leaders and those who serve with them.

May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.

Every time I think of you, I thank my God.

Whenever I pray for all of you, I always pray with joy.

I am so grateful for how you have partnered with me in spreading the good news, from the very first day until now.

I am certain that God, who began this good work in you, will continue it until it is completely finished on the day Christ Jesus returns.

Because you are so dear to me, it is right for me to feel this way about all of you. We share together in God’s grace, both while I am in prison and while I am defending the good news and showing that it is true.

God is my witness how deeply I long for all of you with the tender affection of Christ Jesus.

I pray that your love will grow more and more, guided by true knowledge and good judgment,

so that you can recognize and choose what matters most. Then you will be pure and blameless when Christ returns.

Your lives will be filled with the good fruit of right living that comes through Jesus Christ, bringing glory and praise to God.

I want you to know, brothers and sisters, that what has happened to me has actually helped spread the good news.

As a result, it has become obvious to the entire palace guard and to everyone else that I am in prison because of Christ.

And my imprisonment has given most of the brothers and sisters new confidence in the Lord, so that they now dare to speak God’s message even more boldly, without fear.

Some preach about Christ because they are jealous and want to compete. Others preach with good intentions.

Those who preach out of love know I have been given the task of defending the good news.

The others preach about Christ only to advance themselves. Their motives are not sincere, and they think they can make my suffering in prison worse.

But what does that matter? The important thing is that Christ is being preached, whether the motives are false or sincere. And in that I take great joy. Yes, I will continue to be full of joy.

I know that my distress will become my deliverance through your prayers and the help of the Spirit of Jesus Christ.

My eager hope and expectation is that I will not be ashamed in any way. Instead, I will have complete courage so that now, as always, Christ will be honored in my body, whether I live or die.

Because for me, living means living for Christ, and dying means gaining something even better.

But if living on in this body means more fruitful work for me, then I honestly don’t know which one to choose!

I am pulled in both directions. I long to leave this life and be with Christ, which is far better.

But for your sake, it is more necessary that I remain in this body.

Since I am convinced of this, I know I will stay. I will remain with all of you so that you can grow and experience joy in your faith.

Then, when I return to you, you will have even more reason to be proud of what Christ Jesus has done in me.

Above all, live together as a community in a way that is worthy of the good news of Christ. Then, whether I come and visit you or only hear about you from far away, I will know that you are standing firm, united in one spirit. I will know that you are fighting side by side with a single purpose to stay faithful to the good news.

Do not let your opponents intimidate you in any way. Your courage will be a clear sign to them that they are headed for destruction, but that you are headed for deliverance. This sign comes from God.

For you have been given a gift for the sake of Christ: not only to trust in Him, but also to suffer for Him.

You are facing the same struggle you once saw me face, and that you now hear I am still facing.

Chapter 2

So then, since belonging to Christ gives you encouragement, since His love brings you comfort, since you share a partnership in the Spirit, and since you have deep affection and compassion,

make my joy complete by sharing the same mindset. Share the same love, and work together with one heart and purpose.

Don’t compete against each other or try to make yourselves look important. Instead, be humble and treat others as more important than yourselves.

Don’t think only about what matters to you. Focus on what matters to others, too.

Have the same mindset toward one another that Christ Jesus had.

Though He existed in the very nature of God, He did not treat His equality with God as something to use for His own advantage.

Instead, He chose not to exercise His divine rights. He took on the true nature of a servant and was born as a human being.

And as a man, He humbled Himself in obedience all the way to death—His death on a cross.

Because of this, God elevated Him to the highest place of honor and gave Him the name above every other name,

so that at the name of Jesus every knee will bow—in heaven, on the earth, and under the earth—

and every tongue will openly declare that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.

Because of this, my dear friends, continue to obey as you always have. Now that I am away from you, it is even more important that you do so. Keep living out your salvation with deep reverence and awe.

For God is at work in all of you. He gives you both the desire and the power to live in a way that pleases Him.

Do everything without complaining or arguing with one another,

so that no one can find fault with you. As God’s pure and innocent children, you will stand out in a crooked and corrupt generation, shining among them like stars in the dark sky

as your lives display the message that gives life. Then, when Christ returns, I will have reason to rejoice that my hard work wasn’t wasted.

Your faithful service is like a holy sacrifice offered to God. But even if my life is poured out like an offering over your sacrifice, I am full of joy, and I share this joy with all of you.

In the same way, you too should be glad and share your joy with me.

Trusting in the Lord Jesus, I hope to send Timothy to you soon. Then I will be deeply encouraged when I hear how you are doing.

He is the only one I have who shares my heart and genuinely cares about your well-being.

Everyone else is looking out for their own interests instead of what matters to Jesus Christ.

But you already know Timothy’s proven character. Like a son working at his father’s side, he has served with me to spread the good news.

So I hope to send him as soon as I see how my own situation turns out.

And because I trust in the Lord, I am confident that I myself will come soon too.

But I felt it was necessary to send Epaphroditus back to you. He is my brother, my coworker, and my fellow soldier, as well as the messenger you sent to take care of my needs.

He has been longing to see all of you, and he has been deeply troubled because he knows that news of his illness worried you.

He really was sick and nearly died. But God showed mercy to him, and not only on him but on me too, so that I would not have one heartbreak on top of another.

So I am eagerly sending him back to you. Seeing him again will bring you joy, and it will lessen my own sorrow.

So welcome him with great joy in the Lord, and show high honor to people like him.

His work for Christ nearly cost him his life. He stepped in to serve me in person when distance kept you from doing so.

Chapter 3

And now, my brothers and sisters, rejoice because you belong to the Lord. I do not mind writing these same things to you again. This reminder will help keep you safe.

Watch out for those dogs! Watch out for those who do evil! Watch out for those who insist on cutting the body.

For we are God’s true covenant people. We worship by the Spirit of God, find our confidence in Christ Jesus, and put no confidence in human credentials.

Even so, I myself could rely on human credentials. If anyone else thinks he has reason to trust in them, I have even more.

I was circumcised when I was eight days old. I am an Israelite by birth, a member of the tribe of Benjamin, and a true Hebrew born of Hebrew parents. When it came to the Law, I was a Pharisee, committed to obeying it strictly.

I was so passionate that I fiercely persecuted the church. When it came to the strict behavior required by the Law, no one could find fault with me.

But whatever I once considered profit, I now count as loss because of Christ.

Yes, I consider everything a complete loss because knowing Christ Jesus my Lord is worth far more than anything else. For His sake I have lost everything. I consider it all garbage, so that I can gain Christ

and belong completely to Him. My right standing with God does not come from my own obedience to the Law. It comes from God through my reliance on Christ and is received by faith.

My goal is to know Christ intimately. I want to experience the power that raised Him from the dead, share in His sufferings, and become like Him in His death,

so that, somehow, I may also be raised from the dead.

I do not mean that I have already reached the goal or am already complete. But I keep pressing on to take hold of the purpose for which Christ Jesus first took hold of me.

Brothers and sisters, I do not consider myself to have taken hold of the goal yet. But I focus on one thing: I leave what lies behind and stretch toward what lies ahead.

I press on toward the goal to win the prize that comes with God’s upward call in Christ Jesus.

So all of us who are mature should share this mindset. And if you think differently about anything, God will make even that clear to you.

In any case, we must keep living in a way that matches how far we have already come.

Brothers and sisters, join together in following my example. Pay close attention to those whose lives follow the pattern you see in us.

For I have told you often, and I tell you again now with tears: many people live as enemies of the cross of Christ.

Their end is destruction. Their god is their own appetite. They take pride in what should shame them. Their minds are set on earthly things.

But our citizenship is in heaven, and we eagerly await our Savior from heaven, the Lord Jesus Christ.

He will transform our lowly bodies to be like His glorious body. He will do this by the power that enables Him even to bring everything under His rule.

Chapter 4

So then, my dear brothers and sisters, stand firm in the Lord in this way. I love you and long to see you. You are my joy and my crown, dear friends.

I appeal to Euodia, and I appeal to Syntyche: share the same mindset in the Lord.

Yes, and I ask you too, my loyal partner, to help these women. They struggled alongside me in the work of the good news, together with Clement and the rest of my coworkers. The names of all these coworkers are in the book of life.

Always rejoice because you belong to the Lord. I will say it again: rejoice!

Let everyone see how reasonable and fair you are. The Lord is near.

Do not be anxious about anything. Instead, in every situation, pray to God. Tell Him what you need, ask for His help, and thank Him.

Then God’s peace will stand guard over your hearts and thoughts because you belong to Christ Jesus. This peace is far beyond anything we can understand.

Finally, brothers and sisters, keep your thoughts focused on whatever is true, whatever is honorable, and whatever is right. Also focus on whatever is pure, whatever is lovely, and whatever is admirable—on anything morally excellent or worthy of praise.

Keep putting into practice everything you learned and received from me, everything you heard me teach and saw me do. Then the God of peace will be with you.

Now that your care for me has blossomed again, it brings me great joy in the Lord. You had cared all along, but you just had no opportunity to show it.

I am not saying this because I am in need. I have learned to be content in whatever circumstances I face.

I know how to live with very little, and I know how to live with plenty. I have learned the secret of living in any situation—whether I am well fed or hungry, whether I have plenty or am in need.

I can face all these situations through the one who gives me strength.

Still, you did a good thing by sharing in my hardship.

You Philippians know exactly what happened when I left Macedonia in the early days of the good news. No other church partnered with me in giving and receiving—only you.

Even when I was in Thessalonica, you sent help for my needs more than once.

I'm not looking for a gift. I seek the growing spiritual reward that is being credited to your account.

Now that I have received everything you sent through Epaphroditus, I have all I need and more. Your gift is a sweet-smelling offering to God. It is an acceptable sacrifice that pleases Him.

And my God will meet every need of yours from His glorious riches, because you belong to Christ Jesus.

All glory belongs to our God and Father forever and ever. Amen.

Give my greetings to all of God’s holy people who are united with Christ Jesus. The brothers and sisters who are with me send you their greetings too.

All of God’s holy people here send you their greetings, especially those connected with Caesar’s household.

May the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ dwell with the spirit of you all.
