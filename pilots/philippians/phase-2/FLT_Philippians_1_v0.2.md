### 1
From Paul and Timothy, servants of Christ Jesus.

To God’s holy people in Philippi, all of you who belong to Christ Jesus, together with the leaders and assistants:

### 2
May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.

### 3
I thank my God whenever I remember you.

### 4
Every time I pray for all of you, I pray with joy

### 5
because you have worked alongside me to spread the good news from the first day until now.

### 6
I am sure of this: He who began a good work among you will continue working until it is finished on the day Christ Jesus returns.

### 7
I have good reason to feel this way about you because I carry you in my heart. We share together in God’s grace, whether I am in prison or defending and confirming the truth of the good news.

### 8
God knows how deeply I long for all of you with the tender love that comes from Christ Jesus.

### 9
This is what I pray for you: that your love will keep growing as you gain knowledge and learn to make wise judgments.

### 10
Then you will be able to recognize what matters most and live with integrity and without blame when Christ returns.

### 11
Your lives will be filled with the good fruit of right living that comes through Jesus Christ, bringing glory and praise to God.

### 12
I want all of you to know that my imprisonment has actually helped the good news move forward.

### 13
Everyone in the imperial guard, along with the others here, now knows that I am imprisoned because of Christ.

### 14
Being in prison has made most of the believers here more confident in the Lord. They are more daring to speak God’s message without fear.

### 15
Some proclaim Christ out of envy and rivalry, while others do it with good intentions.

### 16
Those who act out of love know that I am here to defend the good news.

### 17
But the others proclaim Christ from selfish ambition rather than sincere motives. They think they can add to my suffering while I am in prison.

### 18
What really matters is that Christ is being proclaimed, whether people speak with false motives or sincere ones. That is why I rejoice and continue to rejoice!

### 19
I know that my distress will become my deliverance through your prayers and the generous help of the Spirit of Jesus Christ.

### 20
I eagerly expect and hope that I will have no reason to be ashamed. Instead, I will face this with complete courage so that Christ will be honored through me, whether I live or die.

### 21
For me, living is Christ, and dying is gain.

### 22
If I go on living, I can continue doing fruitful work. I do not know which I would prefer.

### 23
I am pulled in both directions. I long to leave this life and be with Christ, which is far better.

### 24
But remaining alive is more necessary for your sake.

### 25
Since I am convinced of this, I know that I will remain and continue with all of you for your progress and joy as you trust Christ.

### 26
Then, when I am with you again, you will have even more reason to rejoice in Christ Jesus because of me.

### 27
The most important thing is that you govern yourselves in a way that reflects the true value of the good news of Christ. Then, whether I come and see you or only hear about you from far away, I will know that you are standing firm, united in one spirit. I will know that you are competing together as one team to stay faithful to the good news.

### 28
Do not be intimidated by those who oppose you. Your steadfast courage is a sign of their destruction and your salvation—and this comes from God.

### 29
For Christ’s sake, you have been given not only the gift of trusting Him but also the gift of suffering for Him.

### 30
You are facing the same struggle you once saw me face and now hear that I am still facing.
