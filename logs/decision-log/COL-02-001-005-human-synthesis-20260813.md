## Final post-smoothing text

1. I want you to know how fiercely I am fighting for you, and for the believers in Laodicea, and for everyone who has never met me in person.
2. I want all these believers to take heart and be bound together in love. I want them to understand deeply and to be completely sure, so that they will come to know God's secret plan, which is Christ Himself.
3. In Christ every treasure of wisdom and knowledge lies hidden.
4. I am telling you this so that no one can talk you into believing something false, no matter how convincing the argument sounds.
5. Even though I am not there in person, I am with you in spirit. I am glad to see how well you are holding together and how secure your trust in Christ is.

## Transparency notes

- `[tn]` Col.2.2: The SBLGNT reads “God’s secret plan, Christ,” identifying Christ Himself as the secret plan. Some manuscripts expand the wording at this point; FLT follows the SBLGNT reading.
- `[tn]` Col.2.5: Paul uses two terms that can evoke a military formation. `τάξις` can describe ordered ranks, and `στερέωμα` can describe firmness or a solid front. Together they picture a community holding its formation under pressure.
