# Colossians 2:1-5 — Human Editor terminal decisions

**Date:** 2026-08-13  
**Human Editor:** David L. Davis

## Final post-smoothing, post-audit-repair text

1. I want you to know how fiercely I am fighting for you, and for the believers in Laodicea, and for everyone who has never met me in person.
2. I am fighting so that all these believers will take heart, bound together in love. I want them to understand deeply, to be completely sure, and so at last to know the secret God kept hidden — Christ Himself.
3. In Christ every treasure of wisdom and knowledge lies hidden.
4. I am telling you this so that no one can talk you into believing something false, no matter how convincing the argument sounds.
5. Even though I am not there in person, I am with you in spirit. I am glad to see how well you are holding together and how secure your trust in Christ is.

## Binding decisions

1. **μυστήριον** — The Col.1:26 precedent `secret plan` is contextually varied at Col.2:2 to **“the secret God kept hidden — Christ Himself.”** This is not abandonment of the Col.1:26 decision. The variation is chosen to make the `ἀπόκρυπτ-` chain audible across Col.1:26 → 2:2 → 2:3. Col.4:3 remains open.
2. **πληροφορία** — Rendered verbally as **“be completely sure”** rather than as an abstract assurance noun. This becomes a precedent to consult at Col.4:12 (`πεπληροφορημένοι`).
3. **σύνεσις** — Rendered **“understand deeply.”**
4. **ἐπίγνωσις** — Rendered **“come to know,”** consistent with the established knowing-language in Col.1:6, 1:9, and 1:10.
5. **πᾶν πλοῦτος (2:2)** — Deliberately not represented by an explicit English phrase in favor of first-hearing clarity. The `πλοῦτος` → `θησαυροί` handoff to v3 is knowingly forfeited. Do not restore it in any later pass without a new Human Editor ruling.
6. **συμβιβασθέντες** — Rendered as the participial phrase **“bound together in love”** riding on “take heart,” not as a coordinate finite clause. Col.2:19 remains open.
7. **ἀγών / ἀγωνίζομαι** — Render contextually rather than uniformly: Col.1:29 remains sealed as **“struggling”**; Col.2:1 is **“fighting”**; Col.4:12 remains open. This supersedes the earlier `contest` lock, which failed the religion-naive vocabulary standard.
8. **Verse 4** — Closed. Three smoothing passes diagnosed a soft cadence, but no proposed repair improved it. **“Talk you into believing something false”** is retained to preserve the deceive-by-reasoning force of `παραλογίζομαι` in transparent ordinary English.
9. **γάρ (2:1)** — Deliberately not rendered. The backward link to Col.1:29 crosses a paragraph boundary and belongs to the study layer.

## Required Transparency Notes

- `[tn]` **Col.2:2 — textual basis:** The SBLGNT reads “God’s secret, Christ,” identifying Christ Himself as the secret. Some manuscripts expand the wording at this point; FLT follows the SBLGNT reading.
- `[tn]` **Col.2:2 — πληροφορία:** The Greek expression translated “be completely sure” describes full assurance or settled confidence, not merely a passing feeling of certainty.
- `[tn]` **Col.2:3 — wisdom and knowledge:** Paul’s repeated emphasis on “wisdom” and “knowledge” in this section likely takes up vocabulary valued by the teachers he is opposing. His point is that every true treasure of wisdom and knowledge is found in Christ.
- `[tn]` **Col.2:5 — formation imagery:** Paul uses two terms that can evoke a military formation. `τάξις` can describe ordered ranks, and `στερέωμα` can describe firmness or a solid front. Together they picture a community holding its formation under pressure.

All four Transparency Notes travel with the exact reading text in the complete-package terminal audit.

## Copyright-gate constrained-phrase flags

The following phrases are recorded for the deferred hard copyright audit as **candidate constrained-phrase exceptions**:

- Col.2:1 **“I want you to know how”** — multiple modern translations converge on this ordinary, source-constrained expression. Convergence across three or more comparison translations is evidence of constraint, not by itself evidence of borrowing.
- Col.2:5 **“I am with you in spirit”** — the Human Editor judges this the only natural English for `τῷ πνεύματι σὺν ὑμῖν εἰμι`.

A named constrained-phrase exception class in the project-wide Copyright Independence Policy is **recommended but not yet adopted** by this unit decision. The deferred hard audit remains required before publication.
