# Colossians 2:1-5 — Human Editor synthesis decisions

**Date:** 2026-08-13  
**Human Editor:** David L. Davis

## Exact synthesis

1. I want you to know how hard I am struggling for you, the believers in Laodicea, and for everyone who has never met me in person.
2. My goal is that their hearts will be stronger and stronger, and bound together in love. Then they will possess the rich confidence that comes from full understanding as they come to know God's secret plan, which is Christ Himself.
3. In Him every treasure of wisdom and knowledge lies hidden.
4. I am telling you this so that no one can talk you into believing something false, no matter how convincing the argument sounds.
5. Even though I am not there in person, I am with you in spirit, and I am glad to see how disciplined your lives are and how firmly you trust in Christ.

## Binding lexical decisions

- `ἀγωνίζομαι / ἀγών`: supersede the earlier `compete / contest` lock with `struggle / struggling` for the Col.1.29–2.1 bridge. The ruling is retroactive to Col.1.29.
- `ἀποκρύπτω / ἀπόκρυφος`: lock `hidden` for the Col.1.26–2.3 literary link.
- `σάρξ`: lock `in person` for the embodied-presence bracket at Col.2.1 and Col.2.5 only.
- `συμβιβάζω`: lock `bind together / bound together` at Col.2.2.

## Note decisions

- `[tn]` Col.2.2: record that the SBLGNT reads “God's secret plan, Christ,” identifying Christ Himself as the secret plan, while other manuscripts contain expanded wording.
- Requested `[sn]` Col.2.5 is routed to `[tn]` under the current three-tier FLT note architecture. The note explains that `τάξις` can describe order or arranged ranks and `στερέωμα` firmness or stability; together they can suggest military formation imagery without claiming that either term is exclusively military.
