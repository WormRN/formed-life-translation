# FLT Decision Log Entry — Colossians 1:28

**Date:** 2026-08-12  
**Status:** confirmed — binding Human Editor override

**Issue:**  
Whether FLT must reproduce Paul's threefold surface repetition of *every person* in Colossians 1:28 or may compress the first two occurrences into normal coordinated English.

**Source form:**  
Paul repeats the universal object with the proclamation sequence: warning every person, teaching every person, so that every person may be presented complete in Christ.

**Decision:**  
Retain the Human Editor's approved wording: “We preach Christ. With all wisdom, we warn and teach every person, so that we may present every person complete in Christ.” The first two Greek occurrences are deliberately compressed to the shared English object “every person,” which grammatically distributes across both coordinated verbs “warn and teach.” The final occurrence remains explicit with “present every person.”

**Executive Override:**  
Blocks or warnings based solely on the absence of three separate English repetitions of “every person” are overruled. A future audit may still block the verse for an actual material loss, addition, or misleading meaning, but not merely for failure to reproduce the Greek surface repetition mechanically.

**Reason:**  
FLT's governing goal is natural dynamic-equivalence English. Repeating “every person” after both coordinated verbs is judged unnecessarily mechanical for the target reader because ordinary English already makes the shared object apply to both verbs. The universal scope is preserved in the warning/teaching clause and repeated again in the purpose clause.

**Final wording:**  
> We preach Christ. With all wisdom, we warn and teach every person, so that we may present every person complete in Christ.

**Related decision:**  
The Human Editor also independently locked *teleios* as “complete” rather than “mature” in this verse.

**Revisit trigger:**  
Only concrete evidence of material semantic loss or misleading meaning, not preference for source-form repetition.
