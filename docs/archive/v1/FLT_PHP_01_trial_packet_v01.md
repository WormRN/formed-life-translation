## 2. Constitution Excerpt for Models

The Formed Life Translation is a dynamic-equivalence New Testament translation from the SBLGNT Greek text. It aims for clear, natural contemporary English, roughly NLT-like in readability, for new believers and readers unfamiliar with church vocabulary.

Translate meaning over form, but use the minimum departure from the Greek required for clarity. Preserve the meaning, logic, tone, and theological force of the Greek. Do not copy existing modern translation wording. The human editor makes the final decision.

---

## 3. Chapter Context

Philippians 1 opens a warm pastoral letter written from imprisonment to a church that has partnered with Paul in the good news.

The chapter moves from greeting, to thanksgiving and prayer, to Paul’s explanation that his imprisonment has advanced the good news, to his reflection on life and death, and finally to an exhortation to live in a way that honors the good news under opposition.

**Tone:** affectionate, joyful, courageous, pastoral, urgent without panic.

**Main literary challenge:** preserve Paul’s warmth and joy while keeping the theological weight of vv. 19–30.

## 6. Known Issues for Trial

### vv. 1–2

- δοῦλοι: servants vs slaves vs bondservants.
- ἅγιοι: God’s holy people vs saints.
- ἐπισκόποις / διακόνοις: overseers/deacons vs leaders/helpers.
- greeting formula: retain “grace and peace.”

### vv. 3–11

- κοινωνίᾳ εἰς τὸ εὐαγγέλιον: partnership in spreading the good news.
- πεποιθὼς: confidence/certainty.
- χάριτος in v.7: grace vs undeserved favor.
- σπλάγχνοις: deep affection.
- δοκιμάζειν τὰ διαφέροντα: discern/recognize what matters most.
- καρπὸν δικαιοσύνης: fruit of righteousness/right living/good fruit.

### vv. 12–18

- πραιτωρίῳ: palace guard vs governor’s headquarters.
- τὸν λόγον: God’s message vs the word.
- false motives vs true ones.
- Paul’s tone in v.18: joy, not resignation.

### vv. 19–26

- σωτηρίαν: deliverance vs salvation; preserve ambiguity if possible.
- μεγαλυνθήσεται: Christ honored/magnified.
- τὸ ζῆν Χριστὸς: “living is Christ” may be retained for force.
- σάρξ: body / this life / flesh.
- ἀναλῦσαι: depart / leave this life.

### vv. 27–30

- πολιτεύεσθε: live as citizens vs live in a way that honors.
- μιᾷ ψυχῇ: one soul / one purpose / united as one.
- πνεύματι: one spirit vs one Spirit.
- πίστις τοῦ εὐαγγελίου: faith of/for the good news.
- ἐχαρίσθη: graciously given / privilege given.
- ἀγῶνα: struggle/battle/contest.

## 14. Trial Success Criteria

The Philippians 1 trial succeeds if it produces:

1. A clearer process.
2. A better packet template.
3. At least a few real improvements to the chapter.
4. A short list of glossary updates.
5. A short list of style-guide updates.
6. A realistic sense of how much work one chapter will require.

The trial does not need to produce a perfect final chapter.
