# FLT Contextual Lexical Matrix — Colossians 2:6–15 Unit Overlay v2.0

**Authority:** Human Editor-approved unit overlay.  
**Engine:** 2.2  
**Scope:** Colossians 2:6–15 only unless an entry explicitly identifies a forward precedent.  
**Relationship to governing Matrix:** This file supplements `FLT_contextual_lexical_matrix_v2.0.md` and the standing Colossians override. It converts the previously registered 2:6–15 stubs into approved unit rulings while preserving unresolved ambiguity where the Human Editor required it.

## παραλαμβάνω — Col.2:6
**Status:** approved contextual ruling.  
Christ Himself is the object; reception of apostolic teaching is a secondary resonance, not a replacement object. Preserve the `as ... so` correspondence between receiving Christ and continuing to live/walk in Him. The teaching link resurfaces in `καθὼς ἐδιδάχθητε` at 2:7.

## ῥιζόω — Col.2:7
**Status:** approved source-side aspect/metaphor ruling.  
Perfect passive: completed rooting with continuing state. Preserve the agricultural metaphor.

## ἐποικοδομέω — Col.2:7
**Status:** approved source-side aspect/metaphor ruling.  
Present passive: ongoing building. Preserve the architectural metaphor and Paul's deliberate mixed metaphor with rooting.

## φιλοσοφία — Col.2:8
**Status:** approved contextual ruling.  
The word identifies the deceptive teaching in this context; it does not authorize a blanket condemnation of philosophy as an intellectual discipline.

## συλαγωγέω — Col.2:8
**Status:** approved source-side metaphor ruling.  
Carry off as spoil / take captive. Preserve the captivity/spoil threat where natural English permits.

## στοιχεῖα — Col.2:8; forward link 2:20
**Status:** approved with deliberately preserved ambiguity.  
**Reading guidance:** `the elemental principles that govern this world`.  
**Live construals:** elementary principles/rudiments; cosmic/elemental powers.  
**Forbidden premature resolutions:** `spirit powers`; `basic teachings`.  
**Transparency note:** required, presenting both readings. The vocabulary change to `ἀρχαί / ἐξουσίαι` in vv10,15 weakens a simple equation with cosmic powers; 2:20's handling/tasting/touching regulations keep the rudimentary-principles reading live.

## πλήρωμα / πληρόω family — Col.2:9–10
**Status:** approved unit relationship; corpus-level English policy remains open.  
Preserve the rhetorical/lexical handoff between Christ's `πλήρωμα` and the believers' `πεπληρωμένοι` state where clear English allows. `Πεπληρωμένοι` is perfect passive and must describe a settled state already brought about, never aspiration or progressive filling. Do not imply believers become divine. The corpus-level πληρ- policy remains explicitly unresolved.

## θεότης — Col.2:9
**Status:** approved source-side sense.  
Full deity / what belongs to being God. Do not weaken the Christological claim merely to avoid theological density.

## σωματικῶς — Col.2:9
**Status:** approved contested stub posture.  
Bodily/incarnational sense is primary, but this hapax's exact range is contested. Preserve bodily content; do not present a finer nuance as certain.

## κεφαλή — Col.2:10
**Status:** approved continuity alert.  
Christ is head over every ruler and authority. Preserve supremacy and continuity with prior Colossians head language without inventing a corpus-wide English mechanism.

## ἀρχή / ἐξουσία — Col.2:10,15
**Status:** approved continuity alert.  
Maintain continuity with Col.1:16 cosmic rulers/authorities unless local English requires a justified variation.

## περιτομή — Col.2:11
**Status:** approved contextual ruling.  
`περιτομῇ τοῦ Χριστοῦ` is circumcision effected by/in union with Christ, not Jesus' infant circumcision. The circumcision is not made by human hands and applies to the believers.

## ἀχειροποίητος — Col.2:11
**Status:** approved source-side sense.  
Not made/done by human hands; distinguishes the Christ-related circumcision from ordinary physical circumcision.

## σῶμα / σάρξ — Col.2:11,13
**Status:** approved contextual ruling.  
At 2:11 `τοῦ σώματος τῆς σαρκός` carries physical-body force and echoes Col.1:22 (`his own physical body`). `Sinful nature` is forbidden at 2:11. At 2:13 the uncircumcision-of-flesh language remains bodily/covenantal in context and does not authorize a generic `sinful nature` gloss.

## ἀπέκδυσις / ἀπεκδύομαι — Col.2:11,15
**Status:** approved word-family alert.  
Removal/stripping in v11 and stripping/disarming in v15. Preserve an English family echo if possible without distortion.

## συνθάπτω / συνεγείρω / συζωοποιέω — Col.2:12–13
**Status:** approved discourse-chain ruling.  
The with-Christ element is load-bearing: buried with Christ, raised with Christ, made alive with Christ. It may not be dropped or varied away for style.

## πίστις + τῆς ἐνεργείας τοῦ θεοῦ — Col.2:12
**Status:** approved working ruling with contested alternative.  
Working reading: faith **in** God's working. Alternative remains live: faith produced by/from God's working. No English construction naturally holds both. Objective reading is only marginally preferred. A Transparency Note is required and neither construal may be presented as certain.

## χαρίζομαι — Col.2:13
**Status:** approved contextual sense.  
Forgive is primary because the object is transgressions; grace-gift coloring may be retained if natural.

## Col.2:13–15 subject continuity
**Status:** approved working construal, not certainty.  
God is the working subject of `συνεζωοποίησεν` and the following chain. A candidate must keep one subject consistently through the sentence; silent drift between God and Christ is a blocking defect.

## χειρόγραφον — Col.2:14
**Status:** approved contextual sense.  
Written record of indebtedness/obligation standing against us. Do not equate it directly with the Mosaic law as a whole.

## δόγμα / τοῖς δόγμασιν — Col.2:14
**Status:** approved preserved syntactic ambiguity.  
The bare dative relation to `χειρόγραφον` is unresolved: the record may consist of the decrees or stand against us by means of them. English must survive either construal rather than confidently choosing one.

## ἐξαλείφω — Col.2:14
**Status:** approved contextual range.  
Erase / wipe out / cancel the hostile written record, preferably retaining the written-record image.

## θριαμβεύω — Col.2:15
**Status:** approved contextual ruling.  
Public victorious triumph over rulers and authorities; Roman-triumph imagery is part of the source rationale, but `triumphal procession` is not required in the reading text.

## ἐν αὐτῷ — Col.2:15
**Status:** contested; working ruling only.  
`Through the cross` is the low-confidence working reading because of v14 proximity. Christ/Him remains a live antecedent. Candidates must not present either antecedent as settled.
