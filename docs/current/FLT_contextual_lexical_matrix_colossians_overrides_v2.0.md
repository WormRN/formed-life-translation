# FLT Contextual Lexical Matrix — Colossians Overrides v2.0

**Authority:** Human Editor-approved overlay to `FLT_contextual_lexical_matrix_v2.0.md`. Where this file conflicts with an older Colossians precedent in the base Matrix, this overlay governs until the base Matrix is consolidated.

**Approved by:** David L. Davis  
**Date:** 2026-08-13

## ἀγωνίζομαι / ἀγών · agōnizomai / agōn

**Status:** approved contextual family precedent  
**Scope:** Colossians 1:29 and 2:1; Colossians 4:12 remains open.  
**Approved contextual renderings:** Col.1:29 `struggling`; Col.2:1 `fighting`.  
**Human Editor ruling:** Render this word family contextually rather than uniformly. The earlier `compete / contest` lock is superseded because `contest` failed the religion-naive vocabulary standard. **Do not alter the sealed Colossians 1:29 wording.**  
**Restriction:** This is not a global one-to-one rendering for the word family; Col.4:12 requires fresh contextual resolution.

## μυστήριον · mystērion

**Status:** approved contextual variation under an existing precedent  
**Scope:** Colossians 1:26 and 2:2; Colossians 4:3 remains open.  
**Approved renderings:** Col.1:26 `secret plan`; Col.2:2 `the secret God kept hidden — Christ Himself`.  
**Human Editor ruling:** The Col.1:26 `secret plan` precedent remains valid, but Col.2:2 intentionally varies the English to make the hidden-language chain audible across Col.1:26 → 2:2 → 2:3.  
**Restriction:** Do not force either English phrase mechanically at Col.4:3; resolve that context afresh.

## ἀποκρύπτω / ἀπόκρυφος · apokryptō / apokryphos

**Status:** approved  
**Scope:** Colossians 1:26, 2:2, and 2:3.  
**Approved rendering family:** `hidden`.  
**Literary link:** Col.1:26 `kept hidden` → Col.2:2 `the secret God kept hidden` → Col.2:3 `lies hidden`.  
**Guardrail:** In 2:3, `hidden` locates the treasures in Christ and must not imply inaccessible elite knowledge.

## σάρξ · sarx

**Status:** approved contextual sense  
**Scope:** Colossians 2:1 and 2:5 embodied-presence bracket.  
**Approved rendering:** `in person`.  
**Sense:** Ordinary physical or face-to-face presence, not the Pauline theological flesh/Spirit contrast.  
**Literary link:** Col.2:1 `never met me in person` ↔ Col.2:5 `not there in person`.  
**Restriction:** This ruling does not govern other σάρξ senses.

## συμβιβάζω · symbibazō

**Status:** approved contextual sense and syntax precedent  
**Scope:** Colossians 2:2; Colossians 2:19 remains open.  
**Approved rendering:** `bind together`, inflected in 2:2 as the participial phrase `bound together in love`.  
**Sense:** The community is joined together in love.  
**Syntax decision:** In 2:2 the participle rides on `take heart`; do not require a coordinate finite clause merely to reproduce Greek surface form.  
**Restriction:** Col.2:19 uses the same verb of the body being held together and requires fresh contextual resolution.

## πληροφορία · plērophoria

**Status:** approved local precedent  
**Scope:** Colossians 2:2; consult again at Colossians 4:12.  
**Approved rendering in 2:2:** `be completely sure`.  
**Sense:** Full assurance or settled certainty, expressed verbally rather than with an abstract noun in the reading text.  
**Precedent:** The verbal solution should be considered at Col.4:12 (`πεπληροφορημένοι`) but is not automatically required there.

## σύνεσις · synesis

**Status:** approved local precedent  
**Scope:** Colossians 2:2.  
**Approved rendering:** `understand deeply`.  
**Sense:** Understanding/comprehension expressed as ordinary verbal English rather than an abstract noun chain.

## ἐπίγνωσις · epignōsis

**Status:** approved Colossians knowing-language precedent  
**Scope:** Colossians 1:6, 1:9, 1:10, and 2:2.  
**Approved rendering in 2:2:** `come to know`.  
**Continuity:** Maintain natural English knowing-language where context permits; do not force one surface phrase mechanically.

## πᾶν πλοῦτος · pan ploutos

**Status:** Human Editor-authorized semantic compression at Colossians 2:2  
**Scope:** Colossians 2:2 only.  
**Decision:** No explicit English phrase is retained for `πᾶν πλοῦτος`; the `πλοῦτος` → `θησαυροί` handoff into v3 is knowingly forfeited for first-hearing clarity.  
**Restriction:** Do not restore an explicit wealth/riches phrase in a later pass without a new Human Editor ruling.

## τάξις · taxis

**Status:** approved contextual sense  
**Scope:** Colossians 2:5.  
**Approved rendering:** `holding together`.  
**Sense:** Ordered arrangement or disciplined formation; in this context the Human Editor preserves the formation imagery in natural English.  
**Note trigger:** `[tn]` explains the possible military association with ordered ranks.

## στερέωμα · stereōma

**Status:** approved contextual sense  
**Scope:** Colossians 2:5.  
**Approved rendering:** `secure`.  
**Sense:** Firmness, stability, solidity; here the believers' trust in Christ is pictured as secure.  
**Note trigger:** `[tn]` explains the possible military association with a solid front or stable formation.
