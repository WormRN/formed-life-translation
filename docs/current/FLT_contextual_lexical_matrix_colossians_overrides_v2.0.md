# FLT Contextual Lexical Matrix — Colossians Overrides v2.0

**Authority:** Human Editor-approved overlay to `FLT_contextual_lexical_matrix_v2.0.md`. Where this file conflicts with an older Colossians precedent in the base Matrix, this overlay governs until the base Matrix is consolidated.

**Approved by:** David L. Davis  
**Date:** 2026-08-13

## ἀγωνίζομαι / ἀγών · agōnizomai / agōn

**Status:** approved contextual family precedent  
**Scope:** Colossians 1:29 and 2:1; Colossians 4:12 remains open.  
**Approved contextual renderings:** Col.1:29 `struggling`; Col.2:1 `fighting`.  
**Human Editor ruling:** Render this word family contextually rather than uniformly. The earlier `compete / contest` lock is superseded because `contest` failed the religion-naive vocabulary standard. **Do not alter the sealed Colossians 1:29 wording.**  
**Restriction:** This is not a global one-to-one rendering for the word family; Col.4:12 requires fresh contextual resolution.

## μυστήριον · mystērion

**Status:** approved contextual variation under an existing precedent  
**Scope:** Colossians 1:26 and 2:2; Colossians 4:3 remains open.  
**Approved renderings:** Col.1:26 `secret plan`; Col.2:2 `the secret God kept hidden — Christ Himself`.  
**Human Editor ruling:** The Col.1:26 `secret plan` precedent remains valid, but Col.2:2 intentionally varies the English to make the hidden-language chain audible across Col.1:26 → 2:2 → 2:3.  
**Restriction:** Do not force either English phrase mechanically at Col.4:3; resolve that context afresh.

## ἀποκρύπτω / ἀπόκρυφος · apokryptō / apokryphos

**Status:** approved  
**Scope:** Colossians 1:26, 2:2, and 2:3.  
**Approved rendering family:** `hidden`.  
**Literary link:** Col.1:26 `kept hidden` → Col.2:2 `the secret God kept hidden` → Col.2:3 `lies hidden`.  
**Guardrail:** In 2:3, `hidden` locates the treasures in Christ and must not imply inaccessible elite knowledge.

## σάρξ · sarx

**Status:** approved contextual sense  
**Scope:** Colossians 2:1 and 2:5 embodied-presence bracket.  
**Approved rendering:** `in person`.  
**Sense:** Ordinary physical or face-to-face presence, not the Pauline theological flesh/Spirit contrast.  
**Literary link:** Col.2:1 `never met me in person` ↔ Col.2:5 `not there in person`.  
**Restriction:** This ruling does not govern other σάρξ senses.

## συμβιβάζω · symbibazō

**Status:** approved contextual sense and syntax precedent  
**Scope:** Colossians 2:2; Colossians 2:19 remains open.  
**Approved rendering:** `bind together`, inflected in 2:2 as the participial phrase `bound together in love`.  
**Sense:** The community is joined together in love.  
**Syntax decision:** In 2:2 the participle rides on `take heart`; do not require a coordinate finite clause merely to reproduce Greek surface form.  
**Restriction:** Col.2:19 uses the same verb of the body being held together and requires fresh contextual resolution.

## πληροφορία · plērophoria

**Status:** approved local precedent  
**Scope:** Colossians 2:2; consult again at Colossians 4:12.  
**Approved rendering in 2:2:** `be completely sure`.  
**Sense:** Full assurance or settled certainty, expressed verbally rather than with an abstract noun in the reading text.  
**Precedent:** The verbal solution should be considered at Col.4:12 (`πεπληροφορημένοι`) but is not automatically required there.

## σύνεσις · synesis

**Status:** approved local precedent  
**Scope:** Colossians 2:2.  
**Approved rendering:** `understand deeply`.  
**Sense:** Understanding/comprehension expressed as ordinary verbal English rather than an abstract noun chain.

## ἐπίγνωσις · epignōsis

**Status:** approved Colossians knowing-language precedent  
**Scope:** Colossians 1:6, 1:9, 1:10, and 2:2.  
**Approved rendering in 2:2:** `come to know`.  
**Continuity:** Maintain natural English knowing-language where context permits; do not force one surface phrase mechanically.

## πᾶν πλοῦτος · pan ploutos

**Status:** Human Editor-authorized semantic compression at Colossians 2:2  
**Scope:** Colossians 2:2 only.  
**Decision:** No explicit English phrase is retained for `πᾶν πλοῦτος`; the `πλοῦτος` → `θησαυροί` handoff into v3 is knowingly forfeited for first-hearing clarity.  
**Restriction:** Do not restore an explicit wealth/riches phrase in a later pass without a new Human Editor ruling.

## τάξις · taxis

**Status:** approved contextual sense  
**Scope:** Colossians 2:5.  
**Approved rendering:** `holding together`.  
**Sense:** Ordered arrangement or disciplined formation; in this context the Human Editor preserves the formation imagery in natural English.  
**Note trigger:** `[tn]` explains the possible military association with ordered ranks.

## στερέωμα · stereōma

**Status:** approved contextual sense  
**Scope:** Colossians 2:5.  
**Approved rendering:** `secure`.  
**Sense:** Firmness, stability, solidity; here the believers' trust in Christ is pictured as secure.  
**Note trigger:** `[tn]` explains the possible military association with a solid front or stable formation.

# Colossians 2:6–15 — Pre-draft lexical stubs

These entries are **source-side stubs, not approved English locks**. They exist so the Passage-Sense gate resolves the important options before A/B/C drafting rather than letting workers improvise them mid-run.

## παραλαμβάνω · paralambanō — Col.2:6

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved.  
**Range to resolve:** receive/accept a person; receive transmitted teaching or tradition; take to oneself.  
**Context pressure:** the object is `Christ Jesus the Lord`, and the following command is to continue walking/living in Him. Determine whether the reception language should foreground receiving Christ Himself, receiving the apostolic message about Him, or a deliberately fused idea.  
**Draft gate:** no English lock until Passage-Sense approval.

## ῥιζόω · rhizoō — Col.2:7

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved.  
**Source force:** perfect passive participle (`ἐρριζωμένοι`), picturing completed rooting with a continuing state.  
**Context pressure:** paired with the ongoing building metaphor that follows; preserve the stability/growth force without forcing Greek morphology into awkward English.  
**Draft gate:** no English lock until Passage-Sense approval.

## ἐποικοδομέω · epoikodomeō — Col.2:7

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved.  
**Source force:** present passive participle, an ongoing process of being built up in Christ.  
**Context pressure:** Paul deliberately mixes rooted-plant and building metaphors. Do not flatten both into one generic growth verb unless the Human Editor explicitly chooses to sacrifice the mixed imagery.  
**Draft gate:** no English lock until Passage-Sense approval.

## φιλοσοφία · philosophia — Col.2:8

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved.  
**Range to resolve:** philosophy/wisdom teaching as the opponents' self-description or Paul's label for their teaching.  
**Guardrail:** this verse does **not** warrant a blanket claim that philosophy as an intellectual discipline is inherently evil; the noun is qualified by `empty deceit` and by its human/elemental rather than Christ-centered source.  
**Note trigger:** likely `[tn]` if the reading text could be heard as condemning all philosophy.  
**Draft gate:** resolve before A/B/C.

## στοιχεῖα · stoicheia — Col.2:8; reappears 2:20

**Status:** **genuinely unresolved contested sense**.  
**Viable range:** basic elements/components; elementary principles or rudiments; cosmic/elemental spiritual powers.  
**Context pressure:** `of the world`, contrast with Christ, the rulers/authorities language of 2:10 and 2:15, and the recurrence in 2:20 all matter.  
**Human decision required:** yes. Do not hide this ambiguity from the Passage-Sense packet and do not let drafting workers settle it independently.  
**Note trigger:** likely `[tn]` whichever reading-text solution is chosen.

## πλήρωμα / πληρόω-family · plērōma / plēroō — Col.2:9–10

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved with binding continuity from Col.1:19.  
**Source force:** v9 says all the `fullness` of deity dwells bodily in Christ; v10 uses the cognate perfect passive `πεπληρωμένοι` of believers in Him.  
**Context pressure:** preserve the rhetorical/lexical handoff between Christ's fullness and the believers' status in Him if natural English permits. Col.1:19 deliberately deferred its fullness note to this passage.  
**Draft gate:** resolve the v9/v10 relationship before A/B/C.

## θεότης · theotēs — Col.2:9

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved.  
**Source force:** denotes deity/divine nature—what belongs to being God—not merely a vague divine quality.  
**Guardrail:** do not weaken Paul's Christological claim; also avoid opaque church jargon if a clear sixth-grade formulation can carry the same force.  
**Note trigger:** likely `[tn]` because the term is theologically dense.  
**Draft gate:** resolve before A/B/C.

## περιτομή · peritomē — Col.2:11

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved.  
**Source force:** circumcision language is metaphorically applied to the believers: a circumcision `not made by hands`, linked with stripping off the body of flesh and `the circumcision of Christ`.  
**Key ambiguity:** `περιτομῇ τοῦ Χριστοῦ` can be misheard in English as Christ's own historical circumcision; determine the genitive relationship and how much clarification belongs in text versus note.  
**Note trigger:** likely `[tn]`.  
**Draft gate:** resolve before A/B/C.

## χειρόγραφον · cheirographon — Col.2:14

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved.  
**Viable range:** handwritten record/certificate; record of debt or indebtedness; written charge/obligation.  
**Context pressure:** it is `against us`, associated with decrees, erased, removed, and nailed to the cross.  
**Guardrail:** do not automatically identify it with the Mosaic law as a whole unless the Passage-Sense evidence warrants that specificity.  
**Note trigger:** likely `[tn]`.  
**Draft gate:** resolve before A/B/C.

## θριαμβεύω · thriambeuō — Col.2:15

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved.  
**Source force:** Roman triumph imagery—lead captives in triumphal procession / triumph publicly over them.  
**Context pressure:** follows the stripping/disarming of rulers and authorities and their public exposure. Determine the natural English way to retain victory/procession imagery for a religion-naive reader.  
**Key ambiguity:** `ἐν αὐτῷ` may point to Christ/Him or to the cross/it depending on antecedent analysis.  
**Note trigger:** likely `[tn]` if the Roman triumph image is compressed in reading text.  
**Draft gate:** resolve before A/B/C.

## συλαγωγέω · sylagōgeō — Col.2:8

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved.  
**Source force:** carry off as spoil / take captive.  
**Context pressure:** the captivity metaphor sharpens the threat posed by the deceptive teaching and may connect conceptually with the triumph/captive imagery of v15.  
**Draft gate:** resolve before A/B/C.

## σωματικῶς · sōmatikōs — Col.2:9

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved.  
**Source force:** bodily / in bodily form.  
**Guardrail:** preserve the embodied character of the claim rather than reducing it to `really` or `fully` without bodily content.  
**Draft gate:** resolve with `θεότης` and `πλήρωμα`.

## ἀχειροποίητος · acheiropoiētos — Col.2:11

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved.  
**Source force:** not made/done by human hands.  
**Context pressure:** distinguishes the believers' Christ-related circumcision from ordinary physical circumcision.  
**Draft gate:** resolve with `περιτομή`.

## ἀπέκδυσις / ἀπεκδύομαι · apekdysis / apekdyomai — Col.2:11, 2:15

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved word-family link.  
**Range:** stripping off/removal in v11; stripping/disarming rulers and authorities in v15.  
**Context pressure:** assess whether Paul intends an audible word-family link across the two verses and whether English can preserve it without distortion.  
**Draft gate:** resolve before A/B/C.

## συνθάπτω / συνεγείρω / συζωοποιέω — Col.2:12–13

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved union-with-Christ verb chain.  
**Source force:** buried together with Him; raised together; made alive together with Him.  
**Context pressure:** preserve the repeated `with/together` participation logic, even if English varies sentence structure for oral clarity.  
**Draft gate:** resolve as a discourse chain before A/B/C.

## χαρίζομαι · charizomai — Col.2:13

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved.  
**Range:** graciously give; forgive.  
**Context pressure:** direct object is all transgressions, strongly favoring forgiveness while retaining the grace-gift coloring if possible.  
**Draft gate:** resolve before A/B/C.

## ἐξαλείφω · exaleiphō — Col.2:14

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved.  
**Range:** wipe out, erase, cancel.  
**Context pressure:** imagery of eliminating a written record before removing/nailing it to the cross.  
**Draft gate:** resolve with `χειρόγραφον`.

## δόγμα · dogma — Col.2:14

**Status:** pre-draft unresolved.  
**Range:** decree, regulation, ordinance.  
**Context pressure:** syntactic relation to `χειρόγραφον` must be resolved before choosing whether the English says a record `with`, `consisting in`, or `expressed in` decrees.  
**Draft gate:** resolve before A/B/C.

## ἀρχή / ἐξουσία · archē / exousia — Col.2:10, 2:15

**Status:** continuity stub with Col.1:16.  
**Source force:** rulers and authorities, now explicitly under Christ's headship and then defeated/exposed.  
**Context pressure:** preserve continuity with the cosmic-power language already established in Col.1:16 unless new context requires variation.  
**Draft gate:** confirm before A/B/C.
