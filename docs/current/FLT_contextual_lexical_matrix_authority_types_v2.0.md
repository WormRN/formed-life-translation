# FLT Contextual Lexical Matrix — Authority-Type Amendment v2.0

**Authority:** Human Editor directive, 2026-08-15.  
**Status:** Governing overlay to `FLT_contextual_lexical_matrix_v2.0.md` until the base Matrix is consolidated.  
**Scope:** All new Matrix entries, packet construction, blind-worker briefs, and Decision Brief review.

## Authority type

Every Matrix entry must carry an **Authority type** distinct from its maturity/status (`stub | working | approved | reopened`). Status answers how settled the entry is; Authority type answers how the entry constrains English.

### LOCKED

Fixed English or fixed audible thread. Departure is a blocking defect. Reserved for terms where the reader must track the word or morphological thread across distance for the argument to work. Each LOCKED entry must state the thread it protects. Adding a LOCKED entry requires a Human Editor ruling.

Current Human Editor-authorized LOCKED set:

- `μυστήριον` — `secret plan`, with approved Col.2:2 contextual variation `the secret God kept hidden`; protected thread: God's formerly hidden purpose disclosed in Christ.
- `ἀπόκρυπτ-` chain — `hidden`; protected thread: Col.1:26 → 2:2 → 2:3 hidden-language chain.
- `παρίστημι` — `present`; protected thread: presentation language across the Colossians argument.
- `νῦν / νυνί` — `now`; protected thread: temporal now/revealed-now movement.
- `συν-` compound participation chain in Col.2:11–13 — the with-Christ element must remain audible; protected thread: participation with Christ in burial, raising, and being made alive.
- `κτίσις` — `creation`; protected thread: the creation bracket linking Col.1:15 and 1:23.

### GOVERNED

Context varies. The entry records what governs the variation, approved renderings with their locations, and the reasoning. Approved renderings are a recorded pattern with accountability, not a menu and not a fixed gloss.

Current Human Editor-authorized GOVERNED examples:

- `ἀγών / ἀγωνίζομαι` — `struggling` at Col.1:29, `fighting` at Col.2:1, Col.4:12 open; governed by whether the construction is a labor clause or standalone assertion.
- `διάκονος` — context/function governs rendering.
- `ἐξουσία` — `rule` at Col.1:13 as singular abstract; `authority` at Col.1:16 as plural class of beings.

### OPEN WITH CAUTIONS

No approved rendering. The entry records traps and boundaries. Workers choose freely within those cautions unless a passage-specific Human Editor ruling narrows the field.

## OPEN WITH CAUTIONS — ἐν Χριστῷ / ἐν αὐτῷ / ἐν ᾧ

**Authority type:** OPEN WITH CAUTIONS  
**Scope:** Corpus-wide unless a passage-specific Human Editor ruling explicitly supersedes it.  
**Force:** May vary by context: sphere, instrument, incorporation, or agency. The reader does not track this construction as a fixed keyword, so it does not require one English rendering.

**Caution 1.** Do not systematically render as `in union with`. That is an interpretive expansion which settles the incorporation reading in every instance, including instances where the force may be instrumental or spatial. Precedent: `in union with Christ` was rejected at Col.1:28 in favor of `complete in Christ`.

**Caution 2.** Default to `in Christ` / `in him`. Any expansion beyond that requires per-instance Human Editor approval.

**Caution 3.** Where `him` would be ambiguous on first hearing because adjacent third-person masculine referents compete, name Christ rather than expanding the preposition. Precedent: Col.2:3.

**Restricted rendering:** `in union with Christ` / `in union with him` unless specifically approved for that individual occurrence by the Human Editor.

## Religion-naive vocabulary boundary for worker briefs

Unpack a term when its **label** blocks comprehension—when a religion-naive reader would supply no meaning or a wrong one (for example `propitiation`, `justification`, `sanctification`, or specialized uses of `righteousness`, `flesh`, `grace`, `glory`).

Do **not** unpack a term merely because its **sense varies by context**. Unpacking a context-sensitive term does not teach the reader; it fixes one construal and forfeits the others. Render such terms plainly and let context carry the force.

When both apply—the label is opaque **and** the sense varies—the term is GOVERNED, not unpacked uniformly. Escalate to the Human Editor rather than improvising a standard rendering.

## Packet-hygiene rule

Packet construction must not convert OPEN WITH CAUTIONS material into a preferred English expansion merely because an overlay or source-side explanation used that expansion descriptively. Before a blind-source packet is passed to workers, packet-build validation must reject restricted English expansions unless the packet records explicit per-instance Human Editor approval.

For the `ἐν Χριστῷ / ἐν αὐτῷ / ἐν ᾧ` entry, packet-build validation must reject worker-facing guidance containing `in union with` unless that exact occurrence carries explicit Human Editor expansion approval.

## Decision Brief drift detection

For each unit, the Decision Brief must report any English rendering of a Greek term or construction that appears **three or more times** without a Matrix entry authorizing that repeated English pattern. Report it as an **observation**, not a translation defect. The purpose is to surface accidental systematization for Human Editor review.

For an OPEN WITH CAUTIONS entry, a repeated restricted rendering is additionally surfaced as an editorial item. It must not be silently corrected in unsealed candidate text.

## Reclassification safeguard

Audit existing Matrix entries using this test: **Would this ruling still be made if the term were first encountered in a different context?** If the answer depends materially on where it was first encountered, report the entry as a possible GOVERNED candidate rather than LOCKED. Audit findings are report-only. No existing entry may be reclassified, including downgraded, without a Human Editor ruling.
