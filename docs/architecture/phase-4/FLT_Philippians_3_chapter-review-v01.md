## Continuous reading text

**1** And now, my brothers and sisters, rejoice because you belong to the Lord. I do not mind writing these same things to you again. This reminder will help keep you safe.

**2** Watch out for those dogs! Watch out for those who do evil! Watch out for those who insist on cutting the body.

**3** For we are God’s true covenant people. We worship by the Spirit of God, find our confidence in Christ Jesus, and put no confidence in human credentials.

**4** Even so, I myself could rely on human credentials. If anyone else thinks he has reason to trust in them, I have even more.

**5** I was circumcised when I was eight days old. I am an Israelite by birth, a member of the tribe of Benjamin, and a true Hebrew born of Hebrew parents. When it came to the Law, I was a Pharisee, committed to obeying it strictly.

**6** I was so passionate that I fiercely persecuted the church. When it came to the strict behavior required by the Law, no one could find fault with me.

**7** But whatever I once considered profit, I now count as loss because of Christ.

**8** Yes, I consider everything a complete loss because knowing Christ Jesus my Lord is worth far more than anything else. For His sake I have lost everything. I consider it all garbage, so that I can gain Christ

**9** and belong completely to Him. My right standing with God does not come from my own obedience to the Law. It comes from God through my reliance on Christ and is received by faith.

**10** My goal is to know Christ intimately. I want to experience the power that raised Him from the dead, share in His sufferings, and become like Him in His death,

**11** so that, somehow, I may also be raised from the dead.

**12** I do not mean that I have already reached the goal or am already complete. But I keep pressing on to take hold of the purpose for which Christ Jesus first took hold of me.

**13** Brothers and sisters, I do not consider myself to have taken hold of the goal yet. But I focus on one thing: I leave what lies behind and stretch toward what lies ahead.

**14** I press on toward the goal to win the prize that comes with God’s upward call in Christ Jesus.

**15** So all of us who are mature should share this mindset. And if you think differently about anything, God will make even that clear to you.

**16** In any case, we must keep living in a way that matches how far we have already come.

**17** Brothers and sisters, join together in following my example. Pay close attention to those whose lives follow the pattern you see in us.

**18** For I have told you often, and I tell you again now with tears: many people live as enemies of the cross of Christ.

**19** Their end is destruction. Their god is their own appetite. They take pride in what should shame them. Their minds are set on earthly things.

**20** But our citizenship is in heaven, and we eagerly await our Savior from heaven, the Lord Jesus Christ.

**21** He will transform our lowly bodies to be like His glorious body. He will do this by the power that enables Him even to bring everything under His rule.

## Review findings

### Oral flow

The chapter moves clearly from warning against confidence in human credentials, through the surpassing value of knowing Christ, into Paul’s pursuit of the goal and his call to imitate faithful examples. The final contrast between earthly-minded living and heavenly citizenship closes with the promised transformation of believers’ bodies.

The human editor approved two concrete oral repairs identified by the whole-chapter review:

- **3:9:** replaced the unclear pronoun “it” with two direct statements identifying the source of right standing with God;
- **3:10:** recast the final sequence with parallel verbs: “experience … share … become.”

The repaired chapter passed its final exact-text-and-notes constitutional audit in GitHub Actions run 30739206096.

### Literary cohesion

The principal chapter links are preserved:

- human credentials in verses 3–6 become loss in verses 7–9;
- knowing Christ in verses 8 and 10 leads into pursuing Christ’s goal in verses 12–14;
- “complete” in verse 12 and “mature” in verse 15 preserve Paul’s deliberate tension;
- Paul’s former pursuit of the church in verse 6 contrasts with his present pursuit of Christ’s goal in verses 12 and 14;
- the mindset of verse 15 contrasts with minds fixed on earthly things in verse 19;
- becoming like Christ in His death in verse 10 anticipates bodies made like His glorious body in verse 21.

### Terminology and reader notes

“Human credentials,” “right standing with God,” “reliance on Christ,” “goal,” “mature,” “mindset,” “citizenship,” and the body-language of verses 10 and 21 remain coherent across the chapter.
