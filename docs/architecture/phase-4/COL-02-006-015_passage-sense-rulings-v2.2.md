## Rulings
1. **2:6 παραλαμβάνω / καθὼς…οὕτως:** Christ Himself remains the object; apostolic-reception resonance is secondary. Preserve the **as received Him / so live in Him** correspondence.
2. **2:8 στοιχεῖα:** preserve both live construals. Approved underdetermined guidance is **“the elemental principles that govern this world.”** Do not resolve as “spirit powers” or “basic teachings.” `[tn]` required presenting both readings.
3. **2:9–10 πλήρωμα / πεπληρωμένοι:** preserve the rhetorical handoff if clear English allows. `Πεπληρωμένοι` is perfect passive, a settled state already brought about; aspirational/progressive renderings are forbidden. The πληρ- family still has no corpus-level English policy. `Σωματικῶς` has bodily/incarnational sense primary but remains contested in exact range.
4. **2:11 περιτομή τοῦ Χριστοῦ:** circumcision effected by/in union with Christ, not Jesus’ infant circumcision. `Τοῦ σώματος τῆς σαρκός` preserves physical-body force and the Col.1:22 echo; “sinful nature” is forbidden here.
5. **2:14 χειρόγραφον τοῖς δόγμασιν:** a written record of indebtedness/obligation against us; do not equate directly with the Mosaic law. `Τοῖς δόγμασιν` is a bare dative; its relation to `χειρόγραφον` remains unresolved. English must survive both live syntactic readings.
6. **2:15 θριαμβεύω / ἐν αὐτῷ:** preserve public victorious triumph without forcing “triumphal procession.” `Ἐν αὐτῷ` is contested; “through the cross” is the low-confidence working ruling because of v14 proximity; Christ/Him remains live.

### A. 2:12 faith-genitive
`διὰ τῆς πίστεως τῆς ἐνεργείας τοῦ θεοῦ` is contested. Objective “faith in God’s working” is only marginally preferred; source/subjective “faith produced by God’s working” remains live. `[tn]` required.

### B. 2:11–13 συν- chain
`Συνταφέντες`, `συνηγέρθητε`, `συνεζωοποίησεν`: the **with-Christ** element is load-bearing and must remain audible in all three.

### C. 2:13–15 subject continuity
God is the working subject construal through the chain, but this is not certain. Each candidate must hold one subject consistently; mid-sentence drift between God and Christ is a blocking defect.

### D. 2:7 participle stack
`Ἐρριζωμένοι` is perfect/agricultural (completed rooting); `ἐποικοδομούμενοι` is present/architectural (ongoing building). Preserve the aspect contrast and deliberate mixed metaphors. `Καθὼς ἐδιδάχθητε` links back to `παραλαμβάνω` in v6.
