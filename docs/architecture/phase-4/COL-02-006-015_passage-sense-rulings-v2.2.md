# Colossians 2:6–15 — Human Editor Passage-Sense Rulings

**Authority:** Human Editor / Executive Editor  
**Engine:** 2.2  
**Unit:** `COL-02-006-015`  
**Status:** approved for blind drafting

## Blindness and copyright boundary

Comparison translations are forbidden from the blind-worker packet. NIV, CSB, NLT, ESV, and NASB may be consulted only after independent A/B/C drafts exist, as a Decision-Brief advisory string-spotting check. Any constrained-phrase exception requires convergence across at least three comparison translations and must be surfaced to the Human Editor; comparison wording never becomes drafting input.

## Approved rulings

### 1. Colossians 2:6 — παραλαμβάνω and καθὼς…οὕτως
Paul foregrounds receiving Christ Himself, while the verb carries the resonance of receiving the apostolic tradition/message through which they received Him. Christ Himself remains the grammatical and semantic object; transmitted-teaching resonance may inform interpretation but may not displace Him.

The `καθὼς…οὕτως` correspondence is the spine of the sentence: **as** they received Him, **so** they are to walk/live in Him. Candidates must preserve the correspondence, not merely translate both halves independently.

### 2. Colossians 2:8 — στοιχεῖα τοῦ κόσμου
The cosmic-powers-only ruling is withdrawn. Both major construals remain live: elementary principles/rudiments and cosmic/elemental powers. Col.2:20 connects `στοιχεῖα` with handling/tasting/touching regulations, while vv.10 and 15 use different vocabulary (`ἀρχαί`, `ἐξουσίαι`) for rulers and authorities.

**Approved underdetermined primary rendering guidance:** `the elemental principles that govern this world`.

`Elemental` is deliberately capable of physical-element and rudimentary senses; `govern` permits impersonal ordering or personal authority. Candidates are forbidden to resolve this as either `spirit powers` or `basic teachings`. A Transparency Note is required presenting both live readings.

### 3. Colossians 2:9–10 — πλήρωμα / πεπληρωμένοι and σωματικῶς
Preserve the rhetorical relationship between Christ's `πλήρωμα` in v9 and the believers' cognate `πεπληρωμένοι` state in v10 when natural English permits. This is not a claim that believers become divine.

`Πεπληρωμένοι` is perfect passive: a settled state God has already brought about. Any rendering that sounds aspirational or progressive (`you can become complete`, `you are being filled`) reverses Paul's argument and is disallowed.

The broader πληρ- family still lacks a corpus-level English policy. Flag the gap; do not let workers improvise a universal policy.

`Σωματικῶς` remains a source-side stub. Incarnational/bodily sense is primary, but the word is a hapax with contested range; candidates may not present a disputed nuance as certain.

### 4. Colossians 2:11 — περιτομή τοῦ Χριστοῦ and τοῦ σώματος τῆς σαρκός
The phrase must not sound like Jesus' infant physical circumcision. Primary source-side sense: circumcision effected by/in union with Christ.

`Τοῦ σώματος τῆς σαρκός` echoes the construction rendered at Col.1:22 as `his own physical body`. Candidates must preserve the physical-body force and may not flatten it to `sinful nature`, which is a theological gloss and breaks the literary echo.

### 5. Colossians 2:14 — χειρόγραφον τοῖς δόγμασιν
Semantic center: a written record of indebtedness/obligation standing against us. Do not identify it directly with the Mosaic law as a whole.

`Τοῖς δόγμασιν` is a bare dative. Its relation to `χειρόγραφον` remains unresolved: the record may consist of the decrees, or stand against us by means of them. Candidates must not smooth the syntax into a confident disputed construction. The English must survive either reading.

### 6. Colossians 2:15 — victory image and ἐν αὐτῷ
Preserve public victorious triumph over rulers and authorities. Roman-triumph imagery belongs to the semantic floor, but `triumphal procession` is not required in the sixth-grade reading text.

`Ἐν αὐτῷ` is **contested**. `Through the cross` is the working ruling because of v14 proximity, but the antecedent chain in vv.13–15 is genuinely tangled. Candidates may use the working ruling only with reduced confidence and must not present the antecedent as settled. A Transparency Note may carry the alternative.

## Additional rulings

### A. Colossians 2:12 — διὰ τῆς πίστεως τῆς ἐνεργείας τοῦ θεοῦ
**Contested.** The genitive may be objective (`faith in God's working`) or subjective/source (`faith produced by God's working`). No natural English wording fully preserves both. Objective is the working ruling, marginally favored by word order. A Transparency Note is required; candidates may not present either construal as settled.

### B. Colossians 2:11–13 — συν- compound chain
`Συνταφέντες`, `συνηγέρθητε`, `συνεζωοποίησεν`: the **with-Christ** participation element is load-bearing and must be audible in all three. It may not be varied away or dropped for style.

### C. Colossians 2:13–15 — subject continuity
God is the working subject of `συνεζωοποίησεν` and, as a source-side construal, of the participial/finite chain following (`χαρισάμενος`, `ἐξαλείψας`, `ἦρκεν`, `ἐθριάμβευσεν`). This is flagged as a construal, not certainty. Each candidate must nevertheless hold one subject consistently through the sentence. Mid-sentence drift between God and Christ is a blocking defect.

### D. Colossians 2:7 — participle stack
`Ἐρριζωμένοι` is perfect and agricultural: completed rooting with continuing state. `Ἐποικοδομούμενοι` is present and architectural: ongoing building. `Βεβαιούμενοι` and `περισσεύοντες` continue the stack. Preserve the meaningful tense/aspect contrast and Paul's deliberate mixed metaphors. `Καθὼς ἐδιδάχθητε` points back to `παραλαμβάνω` in v6; preserve that reception/teaching link.

## Standing constraints carried forward

All applicable locks from Colossians 1:1–2:5 remain governing. In particular: contextual `μυστήριον`; contextual `ἀγών/ἀγωνίζομαι`; the `ἀπόκρυπτ-` hidden chain; `ἐπίγνωσις`; `παρίστημι`; and the πληρ- family. Only locks actually triggered by the source text are to be passed as unit Matrix alerts; earlier decisions remain available as discourse precedent and may not be silently generalized.

The nine additional source-side stubs registered before this ruling are approved for the unit: `συλαγωγέω`, `σωματικῶς`, `ἀχειροποίητος`, `ἀπέκδυσις/ἀπεκδύομαι`, `συνθάπτω/συνεγείρω/συζωοποιέω`, `χαρίζομαι`, `ἐξαλείφω`, `δόγμα`, and `ἀρχή/ἐξουσία`.
