## Reading text

**1** This letter is from Paul and Timothy, servants of Christ Jesus. We are writing to all of God’s holy people in Philippi who are united with Christ Jesus, including your church leaders and those who serve with them.

**2** May God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ give you grace and peace.

## Human editorial decisions

- **δοῦλοι — servants:** Uses accessible reading-view language rather than “slaves” or “bondservants.” The stronger ownership and allegiance sense remains appropriate for the study apparatus.
- **ἅγιοι — God’s holy people:** Avoids the likely religion-naive misunderstanding of “saints.”
- **ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ — united with Christ Jesus:** Makes the defining relationship with Christ understandable rather than reproducing a spatial expression.
- **ἐπισκόποις καὶ διακόνοις — church leaders and those who serve with them:** Uses functional language in the reading text. The recognized-office question and traditional terms “overseers/deacons” remain documented for study notes and final consistency review.
- **Grace and peace:** Retains the established terms while expressing the greeting as an active blessing in natural English.
