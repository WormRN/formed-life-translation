# Philippians 3:7–11 — Required Reader Notes

**Status:** Required notes attached to the sealed human-editor candidate; final chapter-note numbering and placement remain pending.

## Philippians 3:8 — “waste”

The Greek word (*skybalon*) refers to worthless refuse and can even mean filth or excrement. Paul uses deliberately forceful language: compared with knowing Christ, everything he once prized has become worthless.

## Philippians 3:9 — “reliance on Christ”

The Greek phrase (*pistis Christou*) can be understood as either “faith in Christ” or “the faithfulness of Christ.” The FLT follows the first understanding here, reinforced by Paul’s concluding phrase, “received by faith.”

## Philippians 3:11 — “somehow”

This word does not necessarily mean Paul doubts his resurrection; it expresses his humble determination to reach resurrection life by sharing fully in Christ’s life, suffering, and death, leaving the exact path in God’s hands.
