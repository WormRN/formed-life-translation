# FLT Phase 3A — Frozen Benchmark Evaluation Rubric

The benchmark is evaluated only after the blind run is complete.

## Scoring scale

- **2 — Meets or improves benchmark**
- **1 — Legitimate alternative with manageable tradeoff**
- **0 — Material regression or unresolved failure**

## Dimensions

1. **Semantic coverage**  
   Significant Greek propositions, relations, agency, and scope are preserved.

2. **Dynamic naturalness**  
   English is clear, current, oral, and appropriate for the target reader.

3. **Discourse movement**  
   The chapter's logic, emotional progression, and paragraph movement remain coherent.

4. **Meaningful form**  
   Important repetition, contrast, metaphor, emphasis, and ambiguity are preserved or recreated.

5. **Theological restraint**  
   The candidate does not add commentary or erase material theological distinctions.

6. **Matrix and precedent discipline**  
   Weighted terms follow approved contextual policy or are properly escalated.

7. **Copyright independence**  
   Drafting remained blind and no unresolved concentrated overlap remains after review.

8. **Human burden**  
   The system presents decisions rather than forcing the human editor to reconstruct the translation.

9. **Provenance completeness**  
   Inputs, prompts, outputs, revisions, decisions, retries, and hashes are traceable.

## Verdict guidance

- **16–18:** Pass candidate
- **12–15:** Conditional pass; repair production defects
- **0–11:** Fail; do not advance to a new chapter

A zero in blindness, provenance, or safe failure handling triggers an overall fail regardless of total score.
