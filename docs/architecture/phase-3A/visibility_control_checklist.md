# FLT Phase 3A — Visibility Control Checklist

A packet may be released only when every applicable item passes.

## Blind drafting workers

Allowed:

- Greek text;
- morphology;
- lexical range without selected solution;
- neutral context;
- governing rules;
- applicable Matrix entries;
- approved scoped precedents clearly labeled.

Forbidden:

- frozen benchmark English;
- copyrighted comparison wording;
- sibling drafts;
- prior critiques;
- packet-authored preferred translation;
- prior copyright flags;
- proposed finalist wording.

## Self-critique

Allowed:

- its own draft;
- its own packet;
- governing rules.

Forbidden:

- sibling drafts;
- benchmark wording;
- comparison translations.

## Blind cross-critique

Allowed:

- anonymized drafts;
- applicable governing rules;
- source packet.

Forbidden:

- worker identity;
- benchmark wording;
- copyrighted comparison wording;
- human preference.

## Ordinary focused checkers

Allowed:

- candidate wording;
- Greek and context;
- governing materials;
- relevant critique findings.

Forbidden:

- copyrighted comparison wording;
- benchmark wording unless serving in authorized benchmark evaluation.

## Copyright checker

Allowed only after candidate completion:

- candidate wording;
- Greek and context;
- approved comparison set;
- prior copyright-stage findings;
- benchmark only when specifically evaluating the completed run.

## Release decision

Any forbidden-material leak is a hard failure. The packet must be quarantined, logged, rebuilt, and assigned a new packet hash.
